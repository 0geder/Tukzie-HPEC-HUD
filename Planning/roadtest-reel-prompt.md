# Prompt: Instagram reel of a road-test day

Written 21 Oct 2026 for the first road session (`Projects/Roadtest1/`, five phone videos, 131 s in total). Reuse it by changing the folder and the shot list.

## Goal

One vertical video of about 1:30 that tells the day in order: getting the TUKZIE out of the building, into the test venue, a look at the system on board, the challenger (an RC car), and the TUKZIE's run. Fast, clean, readable on a phone with the sound off.

## Rules

1. Story first. Watch every clip (frames every 1 to 2 s) and write a shot list with the in and out time of each shot before editing. Use only footage that exists; if a moment the story needs (for example the RC car racing the trike) is not in any clip, say so and leave it out instead of implying it.
2. Format: 1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 frames/s, H.264 with AAC audio, `+faststart`, under 100 MB. Keep the main action out of the top 250 px and the bottom 400 px, where Instagram puts its own buttons.
3. Pace: cut dead time; speed up walking shots (2x to 3x) and keep the vehicle moving at 1x. No shot longer than about 15 s without a new caption.
4. Captions: short (2 to 6 words), white bold sans on a dark rounded box, upper third, one caption per shot, numbered steps where they help ("Step 1: ..."). Title on the first shot, an end card with the project name. No em dashes.
5. Sound: keep the clips' own sound, sped up with the picture, levelled across clips, with a short fade in and out. Do not add music in the edit; music is added in the Instagram app, where it is licensed.
6. People: the clips show team members' faces and the vehicle's number plate. Posting is the student's decision; get the team's consent first, or blur them.
7. Keep the originals unchanged. Write the output and the working files to `Roadtest1/edit/`, not to the repository (size), and keep the script that made it in the repository so it can be re-run.

## Steps

1. Contact sheets of every clip; shot list with times and captions.
2. Render each shot: trim, speed, scale to 1080 x 1920, caption overlay, audio to match.
3. End card: last frame darkened with the project name.
4. Join, fade in and out, level the sound, check the length (about 90 s) and look at frames from the result before handing it over.
