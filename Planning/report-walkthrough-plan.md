# Report walkthrough plan (22 Oct 2026)

Goal: go through the report together, consolidate what has been captured, list what remains, and wrap up, including where the project goes next. Built on Prof. Winberg's guidance (GRT6 and the student's notes from his session): a good introduction is the first impression and shows you know the field; every table and graph is explained (focus, what, why); video evidence; the conclusion summarises, reflects on the objective, integrates with the rest, and shows captaincy and contingency.

## Step 0: status map (Claude, before session 1)
