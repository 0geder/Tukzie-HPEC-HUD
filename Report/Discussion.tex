\chapter{Discussion}
\label{chap:discussion}

This chapter will interpret the results against the literature reviewed in Chapter~\ref{chap:lit} and the research questions in Chapter~\ref{chap:intro}. Planned themes are: how repeatably low-cost inertial sensors characterise this vehicle's dynamic response, and how strongly that depends on mounting, speed and calibration; whether the chosen time-domain features separate road-induced from powertrain vibration in practice; how the embedded architecture's latency, jitter and data-recovery behaviour compare with what edge-telemetry studies typically report; how the HUD's minimal information hierarchy holds up against the complexity and glance-time concerns identified in the literature; and the practical limits of camera-based distance estimation relative to the time-of-flight measurement.
