\section{Video evidence}
\label{sec:video-evidence}

The videos of the vehicle work are published on YouTube as unlisted videos: anyone with the link or the QR code can watch them, but they are not listed or searchable. Faces of people other than the author and the number plate are blurred, or the people shown agreed to publication. Each entry names the report section the video supports.
