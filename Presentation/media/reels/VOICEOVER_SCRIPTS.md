# Trial reels: voice-over scripts

Four silent 9:16 reels (1080 x 1920, 30 fps), cut from the 15 and 16 October build clips and the 27 October road test. Record the voice-over over each one; the timings match the cuts. About 2.5 words a second reads naturally.

Every number below is from the final report. Text in brackets is a suggested on-screen caption; add it in the Instagram editor so it renders natively.

---

## Reel 1: From the printer to the road (27 s)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 4 | The Prusa printing yellow parts | "I'm building the eyes and ears of an electric trike." |
| 4 to 6 | Printer screen, 90 % | "Every enclosure is printed." |
| 6 to 11 | Assembling the box, sped up | "Inside: an ESP32, a Raspberry Pi and two motion sensors." |
| 11 to 16 | Sensor board in hand | "Each sensor reads the road two hundred times a second." |
| 16 to 21 | Screwing the lid down, sped up | "Close it up, mount it on the trike..." |
| 21 to 27 | The trike driving | "...and take it out on the road." |

[Hook text at 0 s: "Turning an electric trike into a data machine"]

## Reel 2: 3:53 am (27 s)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Desk at night | "It's 3:53 in the morning." |
| 2 to 21 | Building the enclosure, time-lapse | "Final-year project. Two printed enclosures, dozens of wires, a camera, and a deadline. Nobody sees this part. Hours of screwing, re-wiring and testing, so that a trike can measure the road and warn its driver." |
| 21 to 27 | The trike driving | "Eleven days later, it drove." |

[Hook text at 0 s: "3:53 am, final-year engineering"]

The clip is from 16 October and the trike was driven on 27 October, so "eleven days" is right.

## Reel 3: What's inside the box (26 s)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | The Pi and the ESP32 inside the enclosure | "What's inside the yellow box? A Raspberry Pi running a camera detector, and an ESP32 logging the battery, GPS and vibration." |
| 8 to 14 | Camera feed on the laptop | "Its camera spots people and vehicles in about a tenth of a second." |
| 14 to 21 | Map page on the trike's dashboard | "A sensor pod at each end measures how it rides, and it all shows up on the dashboard the trike already has." |
| 21 to 26 | The trike driving | "Twenty-seven watts for the whole system." |

[Hook text at 0 s: "What's inside the yellow box?"]

"About a tenth of a second" is from the Pi 4 median of 118 to 122 ms. Say "about an eighth" if you want to be exact.
